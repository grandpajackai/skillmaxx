### LinkedIn (10:00 AM) · 838 chars
```
One rule we changed in SkillMaxx's first month

We used to mark a tool "broken" the first time its install failed. That turned out to be wrong. Some installs fail at random: a slow network, a registry hiccup, a timing issue. Qodo failed one night and passed the next, and we had already called it broken.

So we changed the rule:
• Broken means it failed two nights in a row.
• One bad night is pending, not broken.
• Pass, fail, pass is labeled flaky and left out of both columns.

The lesson for teams running AI agents: one red result is a signal, not a verdict. Re-run before you rip a tool out, and track flakiness separately so it doesn't hide real breakage or get blamed on the tool's author.

How does your team tell flaky from actually broken?
```
