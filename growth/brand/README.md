# SkillMaxx brand: logo

## Options (vector finals: `*-mark.svg`, `*-lockup.svg`, `*-mono.svg`; previews: `option-*.png`)
| Option | Idea | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| **A: Level-up chevrons** (`option-a.png`) | Double up-chevron, "maxxing" | Crisp at 16px, but it reads as a military rank or "scroll to top". Generic. |
| **B: Check-X ✅** (`option-b.png`) | A checkmark whose long stroke is crossed by a short tail, so it's ✓ and **X** at once | **Chosen.** |
| **C: Rising bars** (`option-c.png`) | Three ascending bars | Clean, but it's the universal signal-strength / analytics icon. Zero ownership. |

## Why B
1. **It says what we do.** SkillMaxx's whole pitch is *tested*. A check is the "Tested ✓" pill that already runs through the UI, so the logo and the product speak the same language.
2. **It hides the name.** The crossing tail turns the check into an **X**, the double-x of Maxx. It's distinctive enough to own and still simple.
3. **It holds up at 16px.** Two 4-unit strokes on a 32-grid, round caps, high-contrast dark on lime. It stays legible in a browser tab (`after/favicon-tab.png`).
4. **It's on brand.** A lime tile on near-black, matching the site accent (#C6F432 / #0A0A0B). The light lockup uses a darker lime (#4D6B00) so "Maxx" stays readable on white.
