# Newsletter CTA rule (approved by Brandon, Nov 1 2026)

Newsletter link: https://skillmaxxs-newsletter.beehiiv.com/subscribe

- About 1 in 3 new Buffer trend posts and Bluesky posts end with the newsletter link, e.g.
  "Weekly install scorecard, free every Tuesday: https://skillmaxxs-newsletter.beehiiv.com/subscribe"
- Track it: if the last 2 posts on a channel had no newsletter link, the next one gets it.
- Never rewrite already-approved/scheduled X copy to add it. Applies to new posts only.
- Never more than one CTA per post. Skip it on replies to other people's posts (no pitching in replies).
- If a post already links a site page, keep that link; the newsletter line goes in place of a second site link, not in addition to two links.
- Site pages (homepage, reports, skill pages, blog, guides, /how-we-test) already carry the inline signup card; no popups, ever.
